margin-mobile: 1rem space-xs: 0.25rem space-sm: 0.5rem space-md: 1rem space-lg: 1.5rem space-xl: 2rem space-2xl: 3rem --- ## Brand & Style This design system establishes an authoritative, sterile, yet deeply human interface tailored for healthcare practitioners, clinical administrators, and patients. Blending Modern Clinical Precision with Soft Institutional Minimalism, the interface prioritizes immediate legibility, reduced cognitive strain during high-acuity tasks, and an environment of trust. Visually, the system avoids both hyper-casual consumer styling and outdated enterprise clutter. It relies on architectural discipline: deliberate structure, hygienic backdrops, crisp low-contrast delineations, and targeted saturations of medical teal to direct immediate diagnostic attention. Micro-interactions are subdued and affirmative, reassuring the operator that critical medical records, schedules, and pharmaceutical dispatches are reliably managed. ## Colors The palette is engineered around clinical confidence and hygienic purity: - **Primary (`#0D9488` / Medical Teal):** The operational anchor. Used for primary actions, active clinical states, confirmations, and focus elements. It provides an immediate calming psychological cue while maintaining robust contrast against light surfaces. - **Secondary (`#131B2E` / Slate Navy):** The structural authority. Applied to high-emphasis typography, critical navigation headers, and authoritative status badges. It conveys permanence and security. - **Tertiary (`#0284C7` / Clinical Cyan):** Reserved for contextual informational alerts, diagnostic links, secondary interactive affordances, and biometric readouts. - **Neutral (`#64748B` / Slate):** Guides secondary metadata, muted iconography, and form labels. - **Canvas & Surface Architecture:** Base canvas renders at `#F8FAFC` (Hygienic Tint), with pure `#FFFFFF` assigned to elevated clinical cards, sidebars, and dialogue modals. Dividers and structural borders utilize `#E2E8F0` to enforce spatial boundaries without introducing visual noise. ## Typography Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometric legibility, wide apertures, and balanced x-height. This ensures critical numbers, dosages, and patient IDs are unmistakable across varied display hardware. - **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and bold weights (`600`, `700`) to anchor dashboards and encounter views. - **Body & Clinical Logs:** Set at regular weight (`400`) with generous line-height ratios (1.4 to 1.5) to allow comfortable reading during sustained chart reviews. - **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated weights (`600`, `700`) and slight tracking expansion (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to maintain legibility in dense data tables, dosage trackers, and status tags. ## Layout & Spacing The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing an adaptive fluid grid model: - **Desktop (12 Columns):** 32px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Allows simultaneous visibility of the navigation rail, patient overview pane, and primary encounter form. - **Tablet (8 Columns):** 24px margin, 16px gutter. Navigation rail collapses to an icon bar; side panels shift to stacked views or flyout drawers. - **Mobile (4 Columns):** 16px margin, 16px gutter. Horizontal table data folds into card-based encounter feeds with thumb-zone quick actions. Spacing tokens (`space-xs` through `space-2xl`) dictate internal component structures and gaps. Dense clinical forms pair with `space-sm` and `space-md`, while major patient modules and panel boundaries separate via `space-lg` and `space-xl`. ## Elevation & Depth This system avoids dramatic dropshadows and heavy blurs in favor of crisp structural clarity and low-contrast borders: - **Border Architecture:** Primary depth is maintained through a 1px solid hairline (`#E2E8F0`) surrounding white cards set over the `#F8FAFC` base surface. - **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F8FAFC`. - **Layer 1 (Cards, Tables, Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadow is either completely flat or accompanied by an ultra-subtle ambient tint: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.04)`. - **Layer 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Flyouts):** Pure `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, with directional lift: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.08)`. - **Layer 3 (Modals, Diagnostic Overlays):** Centered surfaces over a 40% opacity Slate Navy backdrop (`rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.4)`), lifted by `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.12)`. ## Shapes The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** corner geometry. Standard interactive elements and clinical cards utilize `0.5rem` (8px) radius, balancing sterile precision with approachable ergonomics. - **Inputs, Buttons, Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px) border radius to convey reliability and modern clinical efficiency. - **Containers, Modals, Large Panes (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** `1rem` (16px) to `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent modular sections. - **Status Pills & Patient Badges:** Full pill-radius (`9999px`) to immediately differentiate categorical metadata from actionable rectangular fields. ## Components ### Buttons - **Primary:** Medical Teal (`#0D9488`) solid fill with white text, font weight `600`, 8px border radius, 10px 18px padding. Hover state deepens to `#0F766E`. Active state scales subtly (`0.99`). Focus ring displays a 2px offset Teal border. - **Secondary / Outline:** White surface with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), text in Slate Navy (`#131B2E`). Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9` with border `#CBD5E1`. - **Destructive:** Light crimson wash (`#FEF2F2`) with `#DC2626` text and border for triage cautions; solid red (`#DC2626`) for irreversible record purge. ### Cards (Modern Clinical) - Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 8px border radius, and `1px solid #E2E8F0`. - Incorporate a defined header zone with `title-md` weight, optional patient avatar / vital metric badge, and clear metadata rows. - Critical alert cards feature an anchored 4px left-border strip in Teal (`#0D9488`), Amber (`#F59E0B`), or Crimson (`#EF4444`). ### Input Fields & Controls - **Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px border radius, typography set to `body-md` in Slate Navy (`#131B2E`). Focus state transitions border to Medical Teal (`#0D9488`) with an ambient 3px teal glow at 15% opacity. - **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), `#CBD5E1` border when unchecked. When selected, fills with Medical Teal (`#0D9488`) displaying a white vector check or center dot. ### Chips & Badges - **Status Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), 4px 10px padding, `label-sm` typography (all-caps tracked or sentence case). - *Normal/Completed:* `#CCFBF1` background with `#0F766E` text. - *Pending/Observation:* `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text. - *Urgent/Triage:* `#FEE2E2` background with `#B91C1C` text. ### Clinical Lists & Tables - Border-separated rows (`1px solid #F1F5F9`) with alternating hover highlight (`#F8FAFC`). - Header cells styled in `label-sm`, uppercase, color `#64748B`. - Integrated vital signs display tabular figures with contextual unit labels (e.g., `mmHg`, `bpm`) muted in `#94A3B8`. ### Specialized Clinical Components - **Patient Banner:** Fixed or sticky clinical card displaying Patient ID, Name, Blood Group, Age, and Allergy flags with immediate high-contrast visibility. - **Prescription Dosage Tracker:** Segmented inline stepper control using 8px rounded pill increments for dosage scheduling (Morning / Afternoon / Night).
